About Poomala

Poomala is a hidden gem in the cultural heartland of Kerala, where time seems to stand still. Nestled amidst rolling hills and lush greenery, this ancient settlement offers a glimpse into South India's rich heritage and spiritual legacy.

Far from the modern world's hustle, a invites you to experience a way of life that has remained unchanged for centuries, where tradition, nature, and spirituality exist in perfect harmony.

Tourist Attractions

Explore the timeless wonders of Poomala and its surroundings, where every site tells a story of cultural heritage and natural beauty.

Poomala Dam

Poomala Dam is a serene reservoir near Thrissur, surrounded by lush hills and calm backwaters—perfect for peaceful walks, sunset views, and quiet moments close to nature.

Mini Ooty

Mini Ooty in Poomala offers cool breezes, misty hill views, and a calm countryside vibe—earning it the nickname for its Ooty-like charm and peaceful scenery.

Pathazhakkund Dam

Pathazhakkund Dam near Poomala is a quiet, lesser-known spot surrounded by greenery and flowing water—ideal for nature lovers seeking calm, scenic views, and an untouched rural atmosphere.

Vattayi Waterfall

Vattayi Waterfall is a hidden natural cascade near Poomala, best visited during the monsoon, where fresh streams, rocks, and greenery create a refreshing escape into nature.

Pambooram Para

Pambooram Para is a scenic hilltop near Poomala offering wide valley views, cool winds, and a peaceful atmosphere—perfect for sunset watching and quiet nature moments.

Cheppara

Cheppara is a striking rocky viewpoint near Poomala, known for its open hilltop views, cool breeze, and dramatic sunsets—ideal for quick hikes and peaceful escapes.
